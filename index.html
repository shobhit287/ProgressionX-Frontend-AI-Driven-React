What you can do with ProgressionX

  • Log workouts in real time — split-based training, per-exercise set tracking, warm-ups, drop sets, and RIR.
  • See your progression — automatic charts for volume, one-rep-max estimates, body weight, and personal records.
  • AI analysis and coaching — data-driven feedback on your training, recovery, and goals, and plain-English answers to questions about your progress.
  • Weekly splits — build your own or let the app generate one based on your goal, experience level, and training type.
  • Diet logging — track nutrition alongside lifting so training and eating stay in sync.
  • PR tracking — automatic detection of personal records across every exercise.

About

ProgressionX is a web application and progressive web app built for people who want to train seriously and track every rep. The app runs on any modern phone or desktop browser and can be installed to the home screen.
